Work

Work is the room-reading bit. Newsrooms, comment sections, client questions, competitor noise. I listen for the one thing a brand can actually use.

  • Cision / Signal Practice
    Current home. I read coverage, social noise and competitor movement, then turn it into the kind of calm note a team can use.    Now
  • Coca-Cola / Everyday Rituals
    With a brand that big, the interesting thing is rarely scale itself. It is the tiny public ritual: a bottle, a joke, a campaign, a memory.    Culture
  • The LEGO Group / Serious Play
    A brick can carry parents, collectors, nostalgia, creativity and community. The work is noticing which feeling is driving the story.    Community
  • Skyscanner / Travel Weather
    Searches have moods. Dreaming, delaying, comparing, panic-booking, saving for later. I like finding the weather underneath the intent.    Intent
  • Motorsport / Fast Theatre
    Speed is only the surface. Under it: loyalty, rivalry, spectacle, sponsorship, risk, and a fandom that never sits still.    Reputation
  • Health + Wellbeing / Care Language
    A quieter category with sharper stakes. Trust, proof, doubt and reassurance matter more than making the loudest claim.    Trust
  • Tealfeed / Momentum Desk
    The startup chapter. Angles, search, distribution, feedback, content experiments, and learning how fast a small win can teach.    Growth
  • Dab of India / Campaign Floor
    Early agency muscles: calendars, client notes, social behaviour, and the useful pain of seeing which ideas survive the week.    Campaigns
